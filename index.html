Framework

Building outbound from zero, in four steps

Tools come last. Every engagement starts with the revenue number and works backwards.

How many leads does the target actually need?

Change the numbers and the funnel updates. These are typical starting rates, replaced with real ones after the first few weeks of sending.

Starting assumptions: 2% of leads reply with interest, 50% of replies take a meeting, 40% of meetings become opportunities, 25% of opportunities close.

The ICP sits where three things overlap

A good company with the wrong contact goes nowhere. The right person at a company with no urgency doesn't reply. Outreach goes only to accounts in the middle.

Right company

Industry, size, stage, funding, tech stack, and where current customers already are.

Right person

The decision-maker who signs, the champion who feels the pain and the influencer who shapes the shortlist.

Right moment

Two or more signals together: a new leader, hiring for the team, fresh funding, a stack change.

Anti-ICP

Customers, open deals, active pipeline, competitors, founder blocklist, and profiles under 100 connections.

The message is built bottom up

Each layer rests on the one below it. Copy is written only after all five are answered for a persona.

Why now

What changes

2 or 3 outcomes that matter to this persona

What they do today

Spreadsheets, a competitor or nothing, and where it breaks

Who feels it most

The persona with the sharpest pain, in their own words

The problem

What shifted in their market and what ignoring it costs

The top layer is the one prospects notice. It only works if the four layers under it are solid. Most cold email fails because it starts at the top.

Output: one angle per persona and signal, turned into short emails where AI writes the first line from real account data.
